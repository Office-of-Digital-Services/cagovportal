Education rights and laws

{%- include "./immigration-side-nav.html" -%}
On this page
  • Safe school environment
  • Guidance to schools (K-12)

In California, all students have the right to attend public school, participate in activities, and learn free from discrimination. State laws like AB 699 protect student privacy and strictly limit federal immigration enforcement on school campuses. Explore the resources below to learn more about student rights, privacy protections, and how California schools work to keep our communities safe.

Safe school environment

Immigration enforcement

Schools generally cannot voluntarily grant immigration enforcement officers access to campus. They need a judicial warrant or court order.

Personal information

Unless explicitly required by law, schools are prohibited from

  • requesting social security numbers
  • inquiring about the citizenship or immigration status of students, their parents, or guardians.

Sensitive areas

After the Department of Homeland Security rescinded the Sensitive Areas memo, schools are no longer out of reach for immigration enforcement activities. California schools still promote safe school environments and follow model policies. The Governor recently signed legislation, including AB 49 (2025), AB 419 (2025), SB 98 (2025), and AB 495 (2025), that establishes baseline expectations to keep students and families safe by requiring that:

  • Families be notified when immigration enforcement comes on school campuses, and students, their information, and classrooms are protected from ICE and require a judicial warrant or court order to be accessed.
  • Every schoolsite include a “Know Your Rights” California school guide on its website.
