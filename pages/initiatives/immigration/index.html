California’s immigrant communities are vital to the state’s strength and success. People from around the world have made California home, bringing diverse cultures, skills, innovation, and perspectives that enrich our communities and help shape California’s future.

This website provides local governments and service providers with information about state programs and resources to help connect immigrant families with public services, education, legal assistance, and business support.
