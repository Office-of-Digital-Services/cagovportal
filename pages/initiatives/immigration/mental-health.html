California continues to provide essential mental health services for all Californians—regardless of immigration status. Explore these essential services below to learn more.

CalHOPE Warmline

This Warmline provides:

  • Telephone and text-based services in English and Spanish
  • Telephone-based services interpretation

Phone number: (833) 317-HOPE (4673)

988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline

This lifeline provides:

  • Support when experiencing a suicidal, mental health and/or substance use- related crisis
  • Access to a Spanish-speaking counselor 24/7. Call or text 988, then press 2.

Phone number: 988
