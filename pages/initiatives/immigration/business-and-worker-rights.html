California has resources to help businesses and workers understand immigration workplace rights and responsibilities, take steps to prepare, and support employees and business operations. Explore the resources below to learn more.

Employer immigration resources

The California Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development has launched a centralized resource hub to support employers with immigration-related information and guidance.

Webinar: Immigration Compliance for Small Businesses

This training covers:

  • Employer rights
  • I-9 requirements, audits, raids, and policy implementation
  • Privacy and confidentiality
  • Supporting employees after an enforcement incident

What are my rights and responsibilities as a business?

This covers:

  • Judicial warrants and other documentation
  • How to document the encounter
  • Public vs non-public space
  • Responsibilities during I-9 Audit
  • Data protection

Employer Preparedness Guide

This covers:

  • How to build and implement your preparedness plan
  • Detailed information on each item on the checklist
  • Practical guidance, templates, and resources
  • How to establish procedures, assign responsibilities, and prepare your team to respond effectively

Employers’ Legal Rights and Obligations Under California Labor Laws Developed by: CA Department of Industrial Relations (DIR)

This covers:

  • Protections for all workers
  • Employer legal obligations
  • Enforcement reminder
  • How to request training

FAQs: California Workplace Protections for Immigrant Workers

Know Your Rights Under California Labor Laws

This covers:

  • Labor laws that protect all CA workers
  • Where workers can file wage claims
  • Workplace protection information
