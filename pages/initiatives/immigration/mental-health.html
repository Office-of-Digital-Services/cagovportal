California continues to provide essential mental health services for all Californians — regardless of immigration status. Explore these essential services below to learn more.

CalHOPE Warmline

Talk or text with someone who understands stress, anxiety, and depression through personal experience. Get emotional support and help finding additional resources.

988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline

Get support for thoughts of suicide, emotional distress, or a mental health or substance use crisis. You can also reach out if you’re worried about someone else. Support is available in English and Spanish

Find free mental health resources for parents, caregivers, children, and young adults, including coaching, apps, and practical tools for managing stress.
