Public benefits and health coverage

{%- include "./immigration-side-nav.html" -%}

Find health care and food assistance, learn about changes to benefits, and understand how those changes may affect you.

H.R. 1, signed into law on July 4, 2025, changes who qualifies for some benefits and financial help. A separate public charge rule took effect on September 18, 2026. It changes how using certain benefits may affect some immigration cases. Public charge does not determine whether you qualify for benefits. Talk to a qualified immigration legal services provider before stopping or changing benefits because of immigration concerns.

For the latest information and updates, please visit the California Health and Human Services Agency's Public Charge webpage and the Department of Health Care Services' federal policy updates.
